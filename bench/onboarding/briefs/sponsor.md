# Role card: {org}_owner, the sponsor

Held by a person: {owner_name}, the head of {Org}. Kind owner. Written by `of init` from the onboarding package
(`bench/onboarding/briefs/sponsor.md` in the organic-framework repository).

## What the role is

You asked the question the onboarding answers, and you decide on what it finds. Every practice the onboarding
follows puts the decision with one accountable sponsor:
- Block's client, who contracts with the consultant and decides on the findings (*Flawless Consulting*, 4th ed.,
  2023; record §1.2);
- TOGAF Phase A, where the sponsor approves the Statement of Architecture Work (record §1.3);
- Martin and Osterling's executive sponsor, mandatory, who oversees the whole value stream (*Value Stream
  Mapping*, 2014; record §1.4).

"Record" is organic-framework `docs/onboarding-practice.md`.

## What it asks of you

1. **Start your conductor first:** `of spawn {org}_conductor`. It is your one window: tell it what you want, and
   it finds the agent that owns it and follows the request to a result (`docs/conductor.md`). You may still type
   into any agent's pane when you choose to.
2. **Approve the charter** (step 1). The onboarding lead drafts it through your conductor:
   - the scope;
   - the champion;
   - what OF reads and what it does not;
   - where each record is kept (its system of record);
   - the outside parties, each with who writes to it, through which channel and with which access;
   - how findings come back.
   You approve it on one card (an ask).
3. **Tell your last story of confusion or bureaucracy** (step 2): what you could not find out, or whose decision you
   waited for. It chooses where the onboarding looks first.
4. **Answer one decision batch** (step 6). The findings come back as one numbered card, each item with its
   consequence and a recommendation. Your conductor explains each item.
5. **Approve each invasive batch of increments** (step 7): anything that restarts running agents, gives an agent a
   new outside channel, or costs money.

## What OF reads and what it does not

OF reads GitHub, the Claude Code sessions people choose to record, and the structure itself. It does not read your
mail, chat or meetings. A confidential source (the books, a bank, a provider's portal) is read only with your
permission recorded by an ask id (record §8.2 (7)).

## What stays with you

Signing, moving money, identity checks and strategy choices stay with people (record §8.2 (8)). Agents bring them
to you as cards; your conductor never answers a card for you.
