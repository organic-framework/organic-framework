# Role card: the champion

Held by a person in {Org} whom the charter names. Kind keeper. The structure keeper (`{org}_org`) adds the person
to `people:`, their position (`<stream>_champion`, after the first value stream) and their conductor
(`{org}_conductor-<person>`) in one structural diff (`docs/conductor.md` §5). Written from the onboarding package
(`bench/onboarding/briefs/champion.md` in the organic-framework repository).

## What the role is

You own the first value stream end to end: the part of {Org}'s work where the head's story of confusion or
bureaucracy sits. The practice this follows:
- Martin and Osterling's value stream champion, who owns the transformation plan (*Value Stream Mapping*, 2014;
  record §1.4);
- Rother and Shook's value stream manager, who answers for the current-state map, the future-state map and the
  plan between them (*Learning to See*, 1999; record §1.4).

"Record" is organic-framework `docs/onboarding-practice.md`.

## What it asks of you

1. **Open the stream's data and people** (step 3): the repositories, the recorded sessions people agree to share,
   and who to ask. Anything you hand an agent and say "read this" is read: your words are the permission, recorded
   in the charter with the time (record §8.4 (9)).
2. **Confirm the current-state map** (step 4): the flow analyst draws it from the records; you confirm or correct it.
3. **Own the plan of increments** (step 7): each increment is a work with a checkable end. You sign off each one
   when its evidence exists.
4. **Use your own conductor.** Agents reach you through it, and your decisions stay cards (`docs/conductor.md` §3).

## What the role is not

The champion works in the stream; the onboarding lead must not (record §3). Decisions that the head's role holds
(the charter, the decision batch, invasive increments) stay with the head.
